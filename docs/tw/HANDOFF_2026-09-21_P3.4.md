## Operational limits

The old TPEx dividend feed cannot establish current dividend resolutions.
Daily material snapshots are rolling source coverage, not a complete archive or
future earnings calendar. The journal only establishes knowledge from collection
onward. Archive directories use a single acquisition writer; preserve both raw
files and the receipt journal. No background collection has been scheduled.
Event records do not supply corporate-action-adjusted prices. P3.3 remains raw
and unadjusted. There is no new production deployment or stock-workspace UI yet.
